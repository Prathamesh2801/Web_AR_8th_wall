# Brand assets — Zeal Interactive Services (our company showcase)

The Supradyn bottle doubles as our own demo for the company's socials (the client's
brand can't appear there): a removable sticker covers the label, a round one covers
"Supradyn" on the cap. Peel them off and it's the client's bottle again. The app side
is `src/brands/zeal/brand.js`; open it with `?brand=zeal`.

- `Zeal_logo.png`: the logo (green #1C552A on white).
- Sticker look: light label (`#F6F8F3`), deep-green ink (logo `#1C552A`, text `#123D1F`),
  teal `#0B6E63` (links to the bottle's `#05575F` plastic), lime `#8CCB3A` accent lines.
  Green bands top and bottom frame it against the plastic and hide a slightly off cut.
  Icons: Tabler Icons (MIT, `sticker/icons/`). `sticker/bottle-mock.png` = preview on the bottle.
- App UI accent: lime `#9BE23B`, mint `#3FD9A8` (brand.js `theme`).
- Sticker art is GENERATED by `sticker/make-sticker.mjs` (run from the repo root):
  every letter is the Montserrat 800 outline the 3D Typography uses, so "ZEAL" lifts off
  its exact print. Edit the script (layout, services, colours), re-run it, paste the
  printed numbers into src/brands/zeal/brand.js, then `npm run generate-targets -- zeal`.
- Print: `sticker/body-wrap-print.png` (178 x 58 mm incl. bleed, 600 dpi, cut to
  172 x 52) and `sticker/cap-top-print.png` (40 mm incl. bleed, cut Ø 36).
- Still to provide: a hero photo of the bottle with the sticker on

## sticker/

| File | Cut size | Design file (incl. bleed) | 300 dpi export |
|---|---|---|---|
| `body-wrap-template.svg` | 172 x 52 mm (= the label) | 178 x 58 mm | 2102 x 685 px |
| `cap-top-template.svg` | Ø 36 mm (measure: flat cap top − 2 mm) | 40 x 40 mm | 472 x 472 px |

Fill the `ARTWORK` group to the bleed edge, keep logo/text inside the blue safe line,
hide `GUIDES` before exporting.

- **Size = the original label exactly**, so the bottle model and target crops are
  reused as-is. Don't go taller: the shoulder curves in right above the label (wrinkles).
- **Material:** removable, matte, opaque ("blockout") white vinyl, so the old print
  doesn't show through. Never gloss: glare breaks tracking. For many swaps, a matte
  wrap with no adhesive, closed by one removable tape tab at the back seam, works too.
- **Apply:** bottom edge 12 mm above the table, over the old label; ends meet at the
  back (~4 mm gap). Flat, no bubbles or creases.
- **Artwork for tracking:** each coloured window on the template (one 69 mm target
  crop) must be full of high-contrast detail. Avoid big flat colour areas, repeating
  tile patterns, or a lone logo on a plain background. Colours should sit with the
  deep-teal plastic (`#05575F`); the cap disc repeats the logo mark like a seal.
- **After printing:** export the flat body art as `sticker/body-wrap.png` (trim size,
  no bleed, no guides), set its `{w, h}` in `label.px` of src/brands/zeal/brand.js,
  then `npm run generate-targets -- zeal`.
